# Financial Reset Planner — The Reset Ledger

Mode: Operate. Target: `index.html` and all app views. Build path: code.

User authorization, 2026-09-18: replace the generic-looking visual world with a major creative redesign while preserving the product's working substance. Inspiration was reviewed across Godly/Recent.design, SiteInspire, Land-book, Mobbin, and Mobbin Finance+.

THESIS: The Reset Ledger makes financial triage feel like an authored household workbench rather than another pale software dashboard.

WORLD: Warm ledger paper; a matte-black navigation cover; electric chartreuse for the diagnosis, active navigation, and decisive actions; one coral mark for urgency and progress; blue utility links and focus; local Manrope at poster scale. Ruled paper, receipt dividers, square workbook controls, and inline line icons carry the identity without external imagery.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 252px black rail and quiet utility header frame an oversized page title. The monthly diagnosis is a chartreuse poster with a black figure rail; a near-black next-action block shares the row. Ordered next actions and four receipt-like financial facts follow. At 1100px the hero stacks; at 760px the rail becomes Menu and the surface becomes a single pocket-workbook column.

INTERACTION: Initial content appears immediately. User-triggered view and setup-step changes use one 360ms clip-and-saturation ledger arrival. Reduced motion disables it. Keyboard focus follows navigation; mobile users can reach More tools and Data & backup.

FUNCTION: Keep the monthly-first, three-question setup, automatic draft saving, honest unknown states, optional cash detail, saved-data key, calculation rules, guided action engine, import/export, printing, and sample-mode isolation. Detailed income and transaction audits share the selected calendar month; undated legacy entries remain visible but excluded from totals. A completed weekly review upserts one six-number snapshot for that month. Do not migrate or replace stored financial data.

BOUNDARY: `index.html`, `app.js` interface behavior, `action-plan.js`, `logic.js` state compatibility, `styles.css`, the legacy-named `clarity.css`, and design documentation. `styles.css` remains the shared structural and print foundation; `clarity.css` is the single selected production appearance.

ACTION PLAN: One current work order and an optional full 15-step path. Review confirmations remain separate from balance milestones. Preserve note drafts, saved progress, weekly-review resume, explicit debt-list confirmation, and transparent planning defaults. Later steps are previews until their prerequisites are met.
